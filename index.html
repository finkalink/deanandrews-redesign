Newest series

Rhapsody in Browns

After decades with a broad palette, Andrews challenged herself to explore simply the color brown. Not so simple. Tree bark, rocks, coffee, chocolate, her cat Mimi: the possibilities became infinite. These canvases are worked with crushed cacao beans, each holding an array of browns inside and out.

About

Three decades of light

For many years Andrews' work has taken light and color as its subject, drawing on the atmospheric light of coastal landscapes. Her surfaces bring in unexpected materials: glass microspheres, carbon granules, Plexiglas, mylar and, most recently, cacao.
